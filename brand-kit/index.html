Mascot

Soft float, blink every 4s, temple dot pulses.
thinking

Guardrails

Don’ts

The mascot is the voice of trust. Treat her well.

Don't change her colors.
Pink hair + teal headband + glowing temple dot are signature. Recoloring breaks recognition.
Don't stretch or skew.
Always uniform scale. The proportions are tuned for legibility.
Don't drop her on busy backgrounds.
She lives on white, soft, teal, or near-black. Photographic backgrounds kill the silhouette.
