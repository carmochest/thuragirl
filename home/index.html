FAQ

Frequently asked

How is this different from hiring an accounting firm?

Same work, but answers in 3 seconds instead of 3 days. You see live numbers all the time, at one flat price with no per-item extras. Work that legally requires a licensed CPA is coordinated with our licensed partners.

Is my company data safe?

Encrypted in transit and at rest, visible only to you and people you invite. We never sell or share your data, and you can export everything anytime.

I already have a company — can I use it?

Yes — enter your 13-digit tax ID at signup. ThuraGirl pulls your company from DBD and takes over the books immediately.

What do I need to register a new company?

Three name choices, at least 2 promoters with Thai ID numbers, a registered address, and your capital — that's it. Fill it in right in the app, about 10 minutes.

Is there a mobile app?

The web app works fully on phones and desktops today. iOS and Android apps are coming soon.
